# Zadanie: SMART CITY (PKO) — treść i regulamin

Wybrane zadanie (decyzja zespołu, 3.10.2026 po południu). Tekst przepisany z materiałów HackYeah. Zgłoszenie można podpiąć tylko pod jedno zadanie.

## Opis zadania (OPEN TASK)

A city is a system of interconnected services, infrastructure, and everyday decisions. A closed road affects journeys, poorly used spaces limit opportunities for residents, and a lack of up-to-date information makes public services harder to access. Even a small improvement in one of these areas can make life easier for many people.

Your task is to create a solution addressing a specific problem faced by a city or its residents. Choose a situation you want to improve, identify your users, and develop a tool, application, system, or prototype that helps people make better use of urban resources and services.

You may explore areas such as:
- Transportation and journey planning.
- Accessibility of spaces and services for people with different needs.
- Management of energy, water, waste, or shared infrastructure.
- **Communication between residents and public institutions.**
- Using urban data to support decisions.
- Responding to failures, disruptions, and emergencies.

Your project may focus on a single street, neighbourhood, service, or an entire city. Its scale should reflect the problem you choose.

Show how your idea works in a concrete situation and who benefits from it. Consider the accuracy and timeliness of information, ease of use, and the possibility of testing the solution in real urban conditions.
