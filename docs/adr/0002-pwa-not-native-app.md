# One PWA, no native app

Residents need the app both as a website and as an icon on their phone. A PWA gives both from one codebase: it opens from a link, installs to the home screen, and can use the camera and GPS. Native Android/iOS apps would mean two codebases and app-store review, which does not fit a 24-hour build, and the jury could not try the app on their own phones from a link.
