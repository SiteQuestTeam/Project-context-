# Enter the HubMI.pl task, not "Cracow without barriers"

We started with "an app for the city of Kraków" in mind, but the city's own HackYeah 2026 task ("Cracow without barriers", 5 000 PLN, Polish only) is about accessibility of places and routes, not about reporting faults. Fault reporting on its own also scores badly on Idea & Innovation (30% of the jury score), because the mKraków app already lets residents report potholes and broken benches. We chose the HubMI.pl partner task (15 000 PLN): connect residents' needs with knowledge, proven solutions, and people ready to act, and help good initiatives reach the places that need them. Our photo + location + AI sorting core carries over; fault reports become one kind of need among others.

## Considered Options

- **Cracow without barriers**: close fit after turning fault reports into barrier reports, and the city is the jury, but it is a third of the prize.
- **SMART CITY open task**: fits plain fault reporting, but same innovation problem as above.
