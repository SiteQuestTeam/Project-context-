# Enter the SMART CITY task, not HubMI.pl

At the event the team talked to the HubMI.pl stand (ROPS). They do not want bottom-up initiatives from residents; they want a platform that spreads their own ~200 ready innovations among institutions (NGOs, councils, schools) and helps those institutions apply for them. Fitting that would mean turning the product around (institution → ROPS innovation → rollout plan), a new project, and the code rights would pass to Proidea and then to the Region. The SMART CITY open task (PKO, 8 000 PLN) lists "communication between residents and public institutions" as an area, uses the same five judging criteria we planned for, and leaves the copyright with us (rules, point 14). Its mentors ask for one concrete social problem rather than a general platform.

## Considered Options

- **HubMI.pl (15 000 PLN)**: bigger prize, but the partner does not want what we build, and the code rights go to them.
- **Cracow without barriers (5 000 PLN)**: a map of accessible places and routes with source and date for every fact, a different product; the mentor's other idea (score building plans against accessibility rules) is too hard for one night. Code rights go to the city.
